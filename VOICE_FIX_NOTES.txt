Airi voice tuning changes

- Defaults to Gemini 3.8 Flash-Lite TTS for lower speech startup latency.
- Uses the Leda youthful voice preset with stronger anime-character acting direction.
- Carries Airi's reply mood tag into TTS style metadata.
- Reduces waiting in the speech queue and sends completed sentences as soon as punctuation arrives.
- Keeps the flagship Gemini 3.8 Flash TTS model as a fallback only if the preferred model is unavailable.

Note: Voice timbre is still determined by Gemini's selected preset and the model; text style guidance improves delivery but cannot guarantee the same timbre as a previous TTS model. Build and test on a device with Android Studio/Gradle available.
